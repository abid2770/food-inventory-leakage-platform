# Problem Statement

## Background

Many small and medium food manufacturers rely on spreadsheets and manual processes to manage inventory and production.

While these tools are familiar and inexpensive, they provide limited visibility into operational losses and make it difficult to identify leakage before it becomes financially significant.

---

# Business Problems

Organizations commonly face:

* Inventory leakage
* Production wastage
* Stock shrinkage
* Inaccurate inventory balances
* Manual reconciliation
* Data entry errors
* Delayed reporting
* Weak operational controls
* Limited management visibility

---

# Business Impact

These problems lead to:

* Financial losses
* Reduced profitability
* Increased operating costs
* Poor purchasing decisions
* Production inefficiencies
* Customer service issues
* Reduced management confidence

---

# Existing Solutions

Current approaches include:

* Excel spreadsheets
* Manual stock registers
* Basic accounting software
* Expensive ERP systems

These options either lack operational intelligence or are too costly and complex for many SMEs.

---

# Opportunity

There is a significant opportunity to provide an affordable SaaS platform that delivers practical inventory intelligence while integrating with existing Excel-based workflows instead of replacing them.

---

# Desired Outcome

Provide automated rule-based leakage detection, operational monitoring, dashboards, and alerts that enable manufacturers to identify problems early and reduce avoidable losses.
