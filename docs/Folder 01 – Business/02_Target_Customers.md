# Target Customers

## Primary Target Market

Small and medium food manufacturing companies in Pakistan that manage inventory using Excel spreadsheets or basic accounting software.

These organizations typically have limited IT resources and require affordable solutions that improve inventory visibility without replacing their existing workflows.

---

# Ideal Customer Profile

Typical customers have:

* 20–500 employees
* One or more warehouses
* Regular raw material purchases
* Production operations
* Finished goods inventory
* Manual inventory reconciliation
* Frequent stock adjustments
* Limited reporting capabilities

---

# Primary Business Users

* Owners
* Managing Directors
* Factory Managers
* Production Managers
* Inventory Managers
* Warehouse Supervisors
* Store Officers
* Accounts Managers
* Internal Auditors

---

# Common Challenges

Customers often struggle with:

* Inventory leakage
* Production wastage
* Stock shortages
* Overstocking
* Manual Excel errors
* Lack of inventory visibility
* Slow reporting
* Delayed decision-making
* Weak internal controls

---

# Future Expansion

After validating the MVP, the platform can expand to:

* FMCG manufacturers
* Pharmaceutical manufacturers
* Textile manufacturers
* Chemical manufacturers
* Packaging companies
* Distribution businesses
* Warehousing and logistics companies
* International SMEs

---

# Customer Success Criteria

Customers should be able to:

* Upload operational data quickly.
* Detect leakage within minutes.
* Identify inventory discrepancies.
* Improve production efficiency.
* Reduce operational losses.
* Make faster and better business decisions.
